### **Why PEAK Lite?**
Modern Minecraft 1.21.1 modpacks are notoriously demanding on hardware. Dynamic light chunk re-meshing, bloom shaders, and high-poly 3D models can cause severe stuttering on laptops, integrated GPUs, and budget handhelds.

**PEAK Lite** is built specifically to solve this problem. Every single heavy visual engine has been surgically stripped while leaving **100% of the gameplay, progression, questing, and mechanics completely untouched.**

- **Allocates just <span style="color:#2dc26b">5 to 6 GB of RAM</span>** (recommended: 6144 MB) instead of 7.5+ GB.
- **Steady frame rates & fast chunk loading** even in dense biomes and complex kinetic factories.
- **Zero gameplay compromises:** You get all 380+ content mods, full tech trees, magic spells, custom bosses, dimensions, and space exploration.

### ✂️ **What was trimmed for performance?**
To achieve massive frame rate gains and reduce memory consumption, PEAK Lite removes non-essential rendering overhead:
- **Heavy Visual Engines:** Removed LambDynamicLights (chunk lighting recalculations), Shine (bloom shaders), Particular, Visuality, Explosive Enhancement, Dense Flowers, and Cloud Layers.
- **Intensive Entity Animations:** Removed Not Enough Animations, Fancy World Animations, Punchy, and Perception.
- **3D Leaf & Model Packs:** Replaced CPU-heavy 3D leaves with standard fast foliage.
- **Dev & Profiling Overhead:** Stripped background profiling tools like Spark, CraftTweaker, and Panoptic.
- **Retained Core Optimization Suite:** Keeps Sodium, ImmediatelyFast, Entity Culling, More Culling, BadOptimizations, ModernFix, and FerriteCore for maximum raw performance.
